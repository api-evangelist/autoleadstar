A point tool solves one slice of the problem, like an AI chatbot, AI ad optimizer, or AI email sender, but operates on its own. A platform provides the whole marketing operation on one foundation, so the AI can see and act on everything at once. The best AI marketing platform for car dealerships isn't the tool with the most impressive single feature; it's the one that unifies the most of your marketing under one intelligent, data-driven roof.
